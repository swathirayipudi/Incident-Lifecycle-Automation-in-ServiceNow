# ServiceNow Incident Lifecycle Automation

## Project Description

ServiceNow Incident Lifecycle Automation is a project developed to automate and manage the incident lifecycle in ServiceNow.

The project helps in creating, assigning, tracking, updating, and resolving incidents efficiently.

## Project Objectives

- Automate the incident lifecycle
- Improve incident assignment and tracking
- Reduce manual work
- Improve incident resolution
- Provide better visibility of incident status
- # Incident Lifecycle Automation in ServiceNow

## 📌 Project Overview

**Incident Lifecycle Automation in ServiceNow** is an IT Service Management (ITSM) project that demonstrates the complete lifecycle of an incident in the ServiceNow platform.

The project covers the process from initial requirement analysis and service creation to incident creation, classification, knowledge integration, escalation, Level 2 tracking, emergency change requests, child incidents, resolution, knowledge creation, validation, testing, and deployment.

The main objective of this project is to understand and demonstrate how incidents can be managed systematically throughout their complete lifecycle using ServiceNow.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand the complete incident lifecycle in ServiceNow.
- Create and configure the required service and service offering.
- Create and manage incident records.
- Classify incidents appropriately.
- Integrate knowledge resources during incident handling.
- Handle incident reassignment and escalation.
- Track incidents through Level 2 support.
- Create emergency change requests when required.
- Create and manage child incidents.
- Resolve incidents and document the resolution.
- Create knowledge articles from resolved incidents.
- Perform final validation and testing.
- Validate the deployment process.

# 🔄 Project Phases

## Phase 1: Requirement Analysis & Planning

Analyzed the project requirements and planned the complete incident lifecycle process.

The required processes, services, incident handling steps, and validation activities were identified before implementation.

---

## Phase 2: Create New Service & Service Offering

Created the required service and service offering in ServiceNow.

This phase establishes the service structure required for the incident management process.

---

## Phase 3: Incident Record Creation

Created incident records in ServiceNow with the required information.

Incident details are captured so that the issue can be properly tracked throughout its lifecycle.

---

## Phase 4: Incident Classification

Classified incidents based on the appropriate information such as category and other relevant incident details.

Proper classification helps in routing and managing incidents effectively.

---

## Phase 5: Knowledge Integration

Integrated knowledge resources into the incident-handling process.

Knowledge articles can be used to help identify possible solutions and support incident resolution.

---

## Phase 6: Reassignment & Escalation

Handled incident reassignment and escalation when the assigned team or support level could not resolve the issue.

The incident can be moved to the appropriate team for further investigation.

---

## Phase 7: Incident Tracking by Level 2

Tracked incidents that require **Level 2 support**.

Level 2 support can investigate incidents that need additional technical knowledge or troubleshooting.

---

## Phase 8: Emergency Change Request Creation

Created an **Emergency Change Request** when an urgent change is required to resolve or address an incident.

This phase demonstrates the relationship between incident management and change management.

---

## Phase 9: Child Incident Creation

Created child incidents when an incident needs to be divided into related tasks or issues.

Child incidents help in tracking related work separately while maintaining a connection with the parent incident.

---

## Phase 10: Incident Resolution

Resolved the incident after the required investigation and troubleshooting were completed.

The appropriate resolution information is documented before completing the incident.

---

## Phase 11: Knowledge Creation

Created a knowledge article based on the incident and its resolution.

The knowledge article can be used as a reference for handling similar incidents in the future.

---

## Phase 12: Final Validation

Performed final validation of the complete incident lifecycle.

The implemented process was checked against the required project requirements.

---

## Phase 13: Testing & Deployment Validation

Tested the implemented process and validated the deployment.

Different stages of the incident lifecycle were checked to ensure that the expected process was working correctly.

---

## Phase 14: Conclusion

Completed the incident lifecycle project and reviewed the implemented processes.

The project provided practical exposure to incident management and related ITSM processes in ServiceNow.
